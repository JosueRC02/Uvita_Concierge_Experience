Our story

The Heart Behind Uvita Concierge

Behind Uvita Concierge Experience is a passion for tourism, service, and creating meaningful experiences.

With a background in tourism and hospitality, and a deep knowledge of the Uvita area and its surroundings, I've always been drawn to helping people feel comfortable, welcomed, and truly connected to their destination.

After working and gaining experience in the industry, I realized that many travelers were looking for something more than just a place to stay — they wanted guidance, trust, and someone local who could help them make the most of their time here.

That's what inspired me to create Uvita Concierge Experience.

This project was born from the desire to offer personalized attention, connect guests with reliable local providers, and create experiences that feel easy, authentic, and memorable.

Every recommendation, every detail, and every interaction is handled with care, because I truly believe that great service can turn a trip into something unforgettable.

This is more than a service — it's about helping people experience Costa Rica the right way!

— Raquel Chanto
