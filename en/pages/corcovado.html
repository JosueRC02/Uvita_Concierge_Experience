Corcovado National Park

Unique biodiversity and unforgettable nature, in one of the last great tropical rainforests of Central America.

The experience

Biodiversity and wildlife

Corcovado is renowned for its extremely high biodiversity: wild cats, monkeys, tapirs, birds and lush vegetation coexist in a single ecosystem. Hikes are always done with authorized guides and respecting the park's regulations.

Routes, schedules and entry permits depend on the operator and the current regulations of the National System of Conservation Areas. We confirm these details with you before booking.

Recommendations for your visit

These are general recommendations; they do not replace the official guidance of the park or the tour operator.

  • Closed shoes, ideal for humid trails.
  • Reusable water bottle.
  • Light rain jacket.
  • Light, quick-drying clothing.
  • Sun protection.
  • Insect repellent.
  • Comfortable backpack for long hikes.
