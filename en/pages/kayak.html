Water adventure · Costa Ballena

Kayaking in Uvita

Paddle through mangroves, rivers and the South Pacific coast, discovering the nature of Uvita at your own pace.

The experience

Mangroves and sea by kayak

Kayaking is one of the best ways to explore the nature of the Costa Ballena. Paddle through the calm mangroves and rivers of the area, among roots, birds and wildlife, or head out along the Pacific coast off Uvita. There are options for every level, from beginners to adventurers.

We coordinate guided tours with equipment included and local guides who know the best routes according to the tide and season. Tell us whether you want something calm in the mangroves, a trip out to sea or a family experience, and we'll organize it for you.

What the experience includes

  • Kayak and safety gear (life vest and paddle).
  • Experienced local guide.
  • Route through mangrove, river or coast depending on conditions.
  • Options for beginners, families and groups.
